Electricity
=============

The electricity update connects technology-specific generation inventories to
regional supply markets. Generation datasets describe electricity production;
markets combine suppliers for an IAM region, scenario and year, and consumers
are relinked to the appropriate markets.

The generation chapter explains efficiency adjustments and source inventories.
Photovoltaics has a dedicated treatment because its module area, installation
sizes and country yields require distinct assumptions. The market chapter then
explains how suppliers, voltage layers and losses are combined.

See :doc:`/methodology/batteries` for battery manufacturing and technology
assumptions. The treatment of storage in electricity supply is described in
:ref:`electricity-storage-operation`.

# Final three source-3.12 battery decisions

The standing instructions authorize automatic modelling decisions, complete
native screening proxies and scoped unsupported-exchange omissions with warnings.
These decisions prioritize the ordinary workflow without remote upstream
reconstruction. No Python module or test is changed.

Recycled HDPE pellets use the complete native virgin-HDPE granulate process
per signed kg. Polymer identity and nominal finished-granulate mass match;
recycled content, collection, washing, yield, pelletising, allocated recovered
co-products, energy, residues and freight do not. This is an end-product
screening proxy, not a recycling model. It supports no circularity, recycled
content, recycling-benefit, conservative-bound or score-equivalence claim.
The all-solid-state sulfidic-cell input remains 0.0181320 kg per kg cell.
Nothing is renormalized and no additional virgin feedstock or recycling credit
is added. The empty PE recycling handover is not used.

Two unsupported material inputs are explicitly omitted only from their exact
source-3.12 CLIC consumers and the UVEK2026/biosphere3.10 destination:

| Consumer | Omitted material per kg consumer output | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NMC955 cathode | 0.0499464 kg PVDF binder | Binder production and its upstream burdens are missing |
| FKM gasket for VRFB | 0.4443463 kg vinylidene-fluoride monomer | A substantial material feedstock and its upstream burdens are missing |

These are final unsupported-input dispositions under the existing omit-and-warn
authorization. No verified native counterpart was found; hydrogen fluoride,
vinyl fluoride and tetrafluoroethylene are different compounds. No substitute
chemical, zero-burden supplier or recovery credit is added. All other exchanges
remain unchanged. Both consumers remain nonempty, but they are incomplete
inventories and must not be presented as complete material balances or LCIA
estimates. Score effects are unquantified.

Each omission is retained in the audit with the actual current quantity, source
pin, background, original exchange and consumer, and an explicit warning is
appended to the dataset comment. Matching follows the already established
name/reference-product/location rule, not historical hashes. Other consumers,
backgrounds and destinations retain their inputs.

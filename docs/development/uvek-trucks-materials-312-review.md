# Fifteen current truck material, freight and mining inputs

Fifteen reached ecoinvent 3.12 cut-off inputs now convert through the normal
inventory path. Thirteen material-market helpers retain every original producer
entry and final-delivery quantity with its marginal uncertainty, without
grouping or renormalization. Container shipping and the mining proxy use complete
native services directly. The truck retry removes exactly these fifteen
refusals: its current 3.12 group falls from eighteen to three waste markets.
Fourteen of nineteen sectors remain qualified; the truck update is not complete.

Complete native producers replace source production internals. Retained market
shares do not preserve the original regional technology, feedstocks, co-product
allocation, emissions or uncertainty. Warnings explicitly cover these changes:

- Salt retains its two ordinary producers and its rare-earth-oxide and
  methylcyclopropene co-product entries. Fluorspar retains its scandium-tailings
  co-product; pig iron retains its titania and vanadium-related co-products.
  Native complete production replaces those allocated routes. No separate
  primary-product burden or avoided co-product credit is added.
- Ferrochromium remains per kg of the 68%-chromium alloy, and fluorspar per kg
  of the stated 97% product. Neither needs an assay multiplier. The transistor
  represents the same large wired through-hole class, 6.34 g per part, but both
  database references are per kg; no part-count conversion is applied.
- The flexible-foam market retains its 40% high-density TDI, 20% MDI and 40%
  low-density TDI entries, represented by native generic flexible foam. This
  changes chemistry, formulation and density; performance and volume equivalence
  are unverified. It does not authorize substituting neat MDI and TDI elsewhere.
- Native Chinese 70%-stibnite mining/beneficiation is a nominal-kg screening
  proxy for stibnite concentrate. The exact source concentrate assay is not
  established. Source Antimony extraction and native Stibnite extraction use
  different resource definitions; their amounts do not establish an assay
  factor. Contained-metal, resource and score equivalence remain unverified.
- Complete matching polycarbonate, suspension PVC, rigid polyurethane, finished
  LDPE film, liquid argon and 20-pin cable-with-plugs models retain their named
  product forms. Original freight remains separate from native upstream supply.
  Country, fleet, fuel, transport technology and vintage remain proxy warnings.

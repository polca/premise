roles. Each has a positive one vehicle-kilometre reference. The old imported
manufacturing graph is unnecessary for these services: the native diagnostic
imports no additional inventories, creates 78 regional services and 13 fleet
markets, and passes exhaustive graph validation at 12,038 activities.

These preserve Premise's existing role-to-power-class surrogates; they do not
establish a regulatory moped classification or a physical displacement-to-power
conversion. The native NMC variants are representative electric baselines. Keep
their complete vehicle, battery, charging, road, maintenance and wear inventories
once. Do not add an invented chemistry mix or future capacity projection.
Geography, class, operating pattern, vintage, native biosphere exclusions and
score differences remain screening limitations under the user's standing
autonomous approval, recorded for later review.

The source output and canonical Premise market use vehicle-kilometres, while the
actual REMIND arrays declare **billion passenger-kilometres per year**. The shared
Premise algorithm uses normalised IAM service volumes as fleet-share proxies.
It does not perform a per-class occupancy conversion. The native scooter lists
one passenger and the two motorbike classes list 1.1 passengers. Preserve the
native vehicle-km inventories and the shared calculation; do not describe the
IAM production volumes as demonstrated vehicle-distance totals. This existing
share convention and its unknown score effect are now explicit in all six YAML
source limitations. No new occupancy arithmetic has been introduced.

All 78 regional energy inputs match independently calculated IAM relative
efficiency factors. Fixed technosphere service totals remain unchanged after
normal supplier splitting. All 12 non-World market shares match the positive
IAM roles, and all 13 market inputs are nonnegative and normalised. Exact filters
exclude other years, battery chemistries and passenger-km/manufacturing units.
The electricity filter recognises original native grids and the ordinary
low-voltage Premise markets needed after an electricity update. Source notes use
the already shared transport annotation hook. No production Python changes were
needed for these mappings. The selected regression group passes 25 tests.

# Bind the existing advanced steel templates

The real REMIND SSP2-NPi 2050 audit found positive hydrogen-based direct-reduction steel production but no suppliers in its technology map. Existing retained workbook templates had already been imported, filled from reviewed native quantity recipes and regionalised as “other” steel datasets; that did not bind them to the IAM technology used by market construction and efficiency updates.

Seven exact UVEK-target role aliases now bind the existing pairs of low-alloyed/unalloyed workbook templates: conventional BF/BOF with CCS, natural-gas DRI, natural-gas DRI with CCS, hydrogen DRI, top-gas-recycling BF/BOF, top-gas recycling with CCS, and electrowinning. The names are selected from the already qualified steel quantity recipes using each role's existing ecoinvent selector and exclusions. No process, recipe, quantity or technology substitution is added. These are retained foregrounds converted to the selected background, not native BAFU producers or published CLIC records. Their inventory provenance and existing donor/omission limitations remain applicable. No generated-helper exception is needed.

This is a YAML-only runtime change. Exact name, product and kg guards keep the routes separate; CCS variants do not enter the ordinary DRI role. The ordinary primary and secondary native aliases are unchanged. Ninety focused steel mapping, energy and market tests pass, including all fourteen exact suppliers, role separation and existing native exclusions. Ruff and documentation reference drift checks pass. A separate real scenario coverage and exhaustive graph audit is still needed before claiming that these roles are operational or exportable.

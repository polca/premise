# Native steel suppliers outside the ordinary IAM mix

The installed source has nineteen records whose names start with “Steel,”. Five ordinary converter/EAF records are already bound to IAM production roles, and the RER processed low-alloyed mixture now has its own shared market stage. Twelve other records, representing nine distinct names, are not selected by the old lowercase “steel production” filter for the shared other-producer regionalisation step. Their omission is unfinished coverage.

The next alias group should retain complete distinct inventories, without adding them to the ordinary IAM technology mix:

- Electric and converter chromium steel 18/8; three electric alloy grades (44FMn28, 23MnCrSiMoF66, 42CrMoS4); and the native best/worst low-alloyed electric-plant cases. Preserve each grade and its complete operating inventory. Do not average extrema into a typical IAM generator.
- The single reinforcing-steel import production name with four source locations (DE, IT, PL, FR). The inspected records have production inputs and no separate rolling exchange. Review their source-country delivery/production convention before using the unchanged shared geographic regionalisation.
- The CH “Steel, low alloyed, secondary production (100% Rec.)” construction-material product. It buys native electric steel and hot rolling. Preserve both and its recycled-content specification; do not replace it with an IAM mix containing converter steel. Complete-inventory regionalisation is distinct from changing its production mix.

The remaining GLO “Steel, pyrometallurgical processing Li-ion batteries, at plant” is a battery-treatment co-product, not an ordinary steel-production template. Its installed documentation states that the multi-output process allocates 100% of burdens to disposal. The installed steel record has no technosphere inputs. Retain this allocation boundary; do not invent upstream steel-production burdens or pool this identity into an IAM steel role. Confirm the original archive inventory before treating its empty inventory as fully audited, since the installed source also documents biosphere exclusions.

This audit introduces no mappings, quantities, omissions, helpers or runtime changes. Exact aliases for the eligible group, source-boundary verification, shared other-producer regionalisation and regression/export checks remain required before enabling public steel support. The successful combined/advanced-year checks do not substitute for these filters.

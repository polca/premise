# First shipping dependency group

Three ecoinvent 3.9 cut-off correspondences required by the shipping inventories are now independently reviewed. The work changes Brightpath mapping data and Premise approval data. It adds two small supply-mix helpers that connect existing native processes and retain the original market delivery quantities; it creates no new chemical-production inventory or waste-treatment technology. The only Python additions are a maintainer regeneration hook and tests, not runtime sector logic.

The inert-waste treatment reuses an already reviewed helper with exactly the same two inputs and source uncertainty: one kg of generic landfill operation and 1.48e-9 facility units per kg received. Native geography, land use, waste heat, aftercare and age remain screening differences.

The lime market retains three separate producer entries and its original road delivery. Its milled-lime producers actually consume crushed limestone and milling energy without calcination. The European producer's electricity and geography differ from the Swiss native proxy.

No invented mining allocation, recovery credit or performance correction is introduced; score differences remain unquantified.

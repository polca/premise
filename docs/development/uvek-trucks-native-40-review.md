# Forty further truck supplies — 17 November 2026

This batch covers 40 actually required source-3.8 cut-off inputs. Thirty-eight
use existing complete native processes. Two fuel supplies change to a closer
delivered-product boundary: fossil diesel at regional storage and naphtha at
regional storage. All quantities retain factor plus one. No helper, foreground
omission or runtime/generator Python change is introduced.

The electronics matches preserve component family and mounting type. Capacitors,
connectors, diodes, inductors, chips, transformers and transistors remain kg of
the named component. The bare lead-free board remains square metres, excluding
mounted components. Typical component masses, wafer geometry and board mass
are descriptive; they are not extra conversion factors. Native historical
designs, manufacturing updates and performance differences remain limitations.

Several labels needed closer checking. Native nitric acid explicitly references
one kg of pure acid despite its 50%-solution name, so doubling would be wrong.
Both native composite inventories already buy 1.05 kg of glass-filled Nylon 66;
the comment describing a melamine substitute is stale. No replacement inventory
is created. The butyl-acetate comment gives an inconsistent CAS, but its formula,
1-butanol esterification and actual reactants identify n-butyl acetate. That
metadata discrepancy is retained in the review rather than used as a match.

Ordinary native regional diesel contains about 5.5% biodiesel. The selected
explicitly fossil supply avoids that substitution and includes its own refinery
mix, delivery, losses and storage. It is a Swiss geographic proxy for the source
European market; native sulfur specification and performance remain unverified.
Naphtha uses the native European regional-storage supply. No extra distribution,
loss, crude-throughput, LHV, density or combustion multiplier is added.

Maintenance preserves one vehicle-service unit for each 16t/28t/40t lorry.
Brass casting excludes caller-supplied bulk brass. Lamination remains a service
per square metre and excludes the foil. Arc welding remains metres of weld,
with the native warning about its rough quality for welding-important studies.
Liquid oxygen remains kg of the allocated oxygen product, not kg of total air.
Other chemicals, materials and complete supplies retain their native allocation
and documented exclusions, with no extra reaction, concentration or yield factor.

These are functional screening proxies. Source internal producer/delivery
shares and uncertainty are replaced by complete native supplier inventories;
LCIA, grade, design and performance equivalence are not established. Separately
listed caller materials, transport, emissions, formulas and supported uncertainty
remain. Recycled HDPE is deliberately still unresolved: its nonempty recycling
inventory cannot be approved as virgin HDPE by this batch.

Thirty-three Brightpath and eight Premise checks pass, including the previous
35-case regression. All 40 migrated suppliers have finite positive/negative
inventories, 80 signed calculations, without writing a Brightway database.
The normal generator reproduces all three resources byte for byte; both
biosphere resources, all recipes and every prior approval operation are unchanged.

The actual truck import retry reduces its first source cohort from 49 to nine
blocked inputs. These are recycled HDPE plus eight recipe-based inputs covering
waste electronics, wood, polyethylene, potassium hydroxide, PET, benzaldehyde,
wafer fabrication and natural gas. The complete truck update/export remains
unfinished, and the overall verified count remains 14 of 19 sectors. See the

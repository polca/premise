# Complete final-energy supplier coverage through the shared workflow

The combined sixteen-sector REMIND SSP2-NPi 2050 diagnostic now has suppliers
for all 203 positive final-energy roles. Every callback and exhaustive graph
validation passes, with 19,581 activities and zero graph errors. Deterministic
inventory checks cover all 876 activities added by final energy and a genuine
representative of each positive role: 1,894 positive/negative LCIs are finite
and have the expected signed linearity. No Brightway database was written.

Six existing native process classes close eleven active direct gaps: gas-
turbine combustion per MJ, softwood-furnace useful heat, small biomethane-boiler
useful heat, kg coal at mine, kg biomass methanol and kg delivered light fuel
oil. Thirty-six selectors share these same original prototypes across models.
Each native reference is positive one kg or MJ. Technology, geography,
allocation, delivery, vintage and unquantified score differences are recorded;
no quantity, density, efficiency or loss multiplier is invented.

Four missing biodiesel energy-use templates close the remaining fifteen active
roles. They use the complete native used-cooking-oil fuel at the fuelling
station and the existing shared vehicle/equipment normalization. Fuel origin,
waste allocation, delivery, non-CO2 exhaust and nominal FAME/carbon properties
are explicit screening limitations. The legacy product label heat means the
existing inventory per MJ of purchased energy; it adds no useful-heat credit.
Equipment, signs and supported uncertainty come from the normal source vehicle
inventories. No new upstream manufacturing model is built. The callback
reconstructs these templates from the scenario vehicles before export.

The templates are mapping-declared resources, loaded only for requested active
final-energy roles. They do not alter the default three-sector workflow.
Standard electricity, biomass, heat and fuel markets come from the preceding
updates; native country grids are not cloned again to fill prerequisites.
All original ecoinvent selectors, IAM aliases and LHVs remain unchanged.
Two hundred twelve current existing unit/regression checks pass. Earlier
broader rejection/replay-test failures remain separately disclosed; the whole
suite is not claimed green.

# Converter and top-gas-recycling quantity lookups

Eleven more steel workbook recipes are qualified, bringing the total to 12 of the 28 records with replacement metadata. Together they cover 332 of the workbook's 641 lookup slots. They include eight ordinary/CCS/top-gas-recycling converter-steel variants, two top-gas-recycling pig-iron variants, and the alloy auxiliary, in addition to the previously qualified blast-furnace-with-CCS template. These are repeated lookups into existing native producers, not new datasets or independent migration correspondences.

Each recipe identifies the actual native low-alloyed converter, unalloyed converter or conventional pig-iron donor, with a positive one-kg reference. It fills only the source's tagged unknown quantities through the unchanged Premise filler. Explicit source inputs—including pig iron, slag, fuel, electricity, zeolite and capture services where present—remain unchanged. Native amounts use their actual units and reference bases. Gas uses native MJ directly; no inferred m³ quantity, density, purity or metal-content multiplier is introduced. The alloy auxiliary retains its workbook reference and is not normalized into one kg of pure alloy.

The dust and sludge lookup slots jointly borrow the selected native converter's waste-treatment basket once. That basket includes its BOF-waste landfill and EAF-dust landfill proxy. This is an explicit screening substitution for the residue model, not equality of sludge, dust, moisture or landfill emissions. The explicit source slag/coproduct input remains; source/native residue and allocation boundaries may overlap or differ and their score effects remain unquantified. Native 8-bar, <30-kW compressed air differs from the source 600-kPa lookup and carries a pressure/technology warning without an invented pressure multiplier. Native ionic and particle labels are retained as defined in the selected biosphere, without molecular-weight conversion.

There are 74 omitted source-slot occurrences across the 12 recipes because the selected donor lacks the corresponding group. These are repeated occurrences, not 74 independent datasets. Their source quantities are unknown, not measured zero, and their score effects are unquantified. Every omission remains visible in the warning/provenance audit. Additional native input groups outside the source template's slots are not silently added; this is not a complete native donor copy or source-inventory equivalence. Conventional donor emissions borrowed for top-gas recycling are not recalculated from its explicit technology-specific fuel inputs. Native source import exclusions and unchanged donor-quantity uncertainty limits remain documented.

One small Python adaptation permits an explicitly declared per-slot donor-reference alias. The source unalloyed templates contain two reference-product spellings for the same named RER converter. Each exact observed spelling is declared in YAML; arbitrary spelling changes still fail. All other expansion is mapping data. YAML anchors deduplicate identical lookup definitions without changing their parsed content or the original recipe.

Validation: all 12 recipes pass against the actual installed native donors; every explicit non-placeholder exchange remains unchanged. Thirty-nine focused/native-workbook/user/hybrid regressions pass. Twenty-four signed finite LCIs pass for the filled components, including direct elementary flows, without changing the native database. These component calculations exclude explicit workbook exchanges and do not establish a complete technology inventory, steel transformation, certification, export or source/native score equivalence.

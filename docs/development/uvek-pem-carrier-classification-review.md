# Preserve hydrogen classification when selecting PEM production

A normal public nine-sector update exposed an interaction missed by the fuel-only check. The earlier PEM correction correctly selected a production plant instead of a complete pipeline-delivery inventory for the fuel-market production mix. However, the same alias also supplies the default fuel classification used by heat. Removing delivered PEM hydrogen from that default made the native hydrogen boiler overlook its fuel and derive a delivered-heat factor of 378.4 from its small auxiliary electricity input. The strict heat guard correctly refused it.

One YAML entry now uses the existing distinction between classification and market supply. Its default exact aliases include native PEM production and delivered PEM hydrogen. Its `market_suppliers` override selects only the production plant. Heat therefore recognizes the actual delivered hydrogen input, while the fuel market still adds distribution only once. There is no new runtime branch, efficiency formula, numerical override, inventory reconstruction or helper. The existing reviewed source boundary states 120 MJ/kg and retains both original positive fuel and distribution-loss inputs.

Three regressions failed before the mapping correction: carrier classification in ordinary and indexed stores, and the shared heat conversion with hydrogen plus a small electricity auxiliary. After correction all 189 fuel-filter, heat, energy, hydrogen and exhaust regressions pass. They verify that production selection excludes the delivery process, classification retains it, both positive fuel inputs are counted and exchange coefficients remain unchanged. Combined public-workflow and export qualification is recorded separately; this mapping check alone does not qualify another sector.

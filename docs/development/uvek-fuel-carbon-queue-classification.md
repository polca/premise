# Remaining native fuel-carbon inspection queue

The latest actual REMIND five-sector export has 927 native operating consumers
covered by the current selectors, 235 unreviewed process candidates and 64 delivery
carriers in the bounded scan. Selection requires a positive purchased fuel with
forwarding properties and positive CO2, methane or CO emissions. It is not a count
of confirmed errors, and does not cover every converted helper or future clone.

All 64 delivery-carrier records are native low-pressure gas supplies. Independent
inspection verifies their outgoing one-MJ carbon properties against their actual
incoming delivered-gas properties. Their complete exchange multisets agree with
the preceding allocated-heat/carbon export, including network infrastructure,
operating service, all quantities, signs, uncertainty and emissions. All 128
positive/negative unit-demand LCIs are finite with exact sign reversal. No input
or loss is zeroed or removed. The aggregate receipt is
`uvek-low-pressure-carrier-boundary-verification.json`.

This verifies the existing explicit boundary: fuel composition is propagated,
while native methane and other non-CO2 loss-flow origins remain unchanged. It
neither proves full future gas-composition equivalence nor claims to have updated
all loss-emission origins. That limitation remains visible; these 64 records
should not be described as 64 missing carrier mappings.

The 235 process candidates mix genuine operating services, manufacturing,
feedstock chemistry, source-gas production, industrial/process carbon and
mixed-fuel inventories. Their complete CO2 pools cannot safely be assigned to
the directly purchased fuel. The private exact-identity queue remains available
for further primary review. No blanket selector or full-oxidation assumption
is authorized by this classification.

The separate steel preparation diagnostic exposes four unreviewed correspondences
in its first ecoinvent 3.9 batch. See `uvek-steel-first-preparation-frontier.json`.
The delivered-gas-to-venting suggestion is physically wrong and stays refused;
the zeolite treatment has opposite source/native reference signs. Neither is
bypassed. Later profiles and transformation/filter coverage remain untested.

No runtime or mapping behavior changes in this inspection milestone. The private
diagnostics used the existing unrelated local GAINS edits and bypassed only the
public sector-support guards. Migration and exhaustive graph validation remain
active. Public fuels/steel support and the wider integration are unfinished.

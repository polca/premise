# Reference basis of nested omission audits

A propane-machine migration exposed a generic audit failure. Its service reference is one MJ, but two reviewed waste-oil child recipes each represent one kg. Their four existing production-only clinker-treatment handoffs are audited per kg. Premise previously compared every omission with the enclosing matched correspondence's reference unit, so the valid child audits were rejected as unapproved losses.

Changing aliases or migration quantities cannot fix that comparison without mislabelling the service as kg, changing the waste quantities, discarding the reviewed treatment mix, or weakening loss validation. The necessary runtime adaptation is confined to `approved_recipe_source_omission` in `premise/inventory/clic_rule_approvals.py`: also inspect explicitly packaged `nested_recipe_inspections`, keeping each child's own source profile, reference basis and literal omission list. The enclosing matched correspondence's existing fingerprint binds the full helper closure; its packaged review records the exact inherited child authorities. Ordinary top-level audits retain their previous behavior.

There is no global loss-code exemption or implicit approval of other child recipes. An unlisted child, unmatched parent, changed omission/index, source release, unit or reference quantity remains refused. Thirteen focused unit checks cover acceptance and those refusals. End-to-end propane tests exercise signs, uncertainty, cold/warm cache and the four inherited audits; existing quantity-drift and literal-omission guards are also checked.

Primary read-only inspection reconfirmed all four clinker-treatment terminals have negative-one-kg references, no nonproduction exchanges and unchanged inventory hashes. These are allocated handovers with zero recorded LCI, not a claim that physical kiln treatment is burden-free. Their original signed quantities and fractions stay in the audit. No new omission, renormalization or recovery credit is introduced. The propane decision and verification files record its separate modelling limitations.

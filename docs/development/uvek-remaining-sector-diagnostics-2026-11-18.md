# Remaining four sectors: operational evidence, 18 November

Fifteen of nineteen sectors remain qualified through the ordinary workflow and
Brightway export. No new sector is declared supported by these diagnostics.
No repository Python source or test changed and no database was written.

Final energy clears twelve source-3.9 support mappings, including one immediate
heat market retaining all 29 source shares. The next preparation failure reports
five source-3.8 correspondences: gaseous hydrogen, Chinese low-voltage electricity,
bus disposal, bus maintenance and a passenger-car inverter. These need exact
reference-basis review, not a new deep manufacturing reconstruction. The shared
final-energy callback also still defaults to ecoinvent family context; this must
be assessed after preparation rather than hidden by a successful mapping test.

The actual shared metals update passes graph validation at both 2050 and 2030.
At 2050 it preserves 103 markets because the share dataset ends in 2030. At 2030
it creates 50 activities and records 77 transformed targets. It also preserves
51 markets for missing country transport and 48 for missing suppliers. These
are unfinished filter/coverage cases; missing supplier labels do not prove that
native physical processes are absent. Pure titanium and cerium/yttrium inputs
also lack verified providers. Rutile or a cerium concentrate cannot silently
stand for the pure metal or oxide. Atomic ceramic-design updates remain together.
No missing projection is replaced by an invented future trajectory.

The existing native emissions callback creates 314 caller-owned variants and
relinks 2,918 callers, with no exhaustive graph errors. Its own completeness flag
remains false. It covers reviewed combustion gases; other pollutants and retained
scopes still require coverage checks. This partial result is not full support.

Battery mappings prepare successfully. The separate four-file compatibility
proposal passes its private preview and 92 existing tests but remains unapplied
under the user's no-source-change instruction. Public battery update, export and
combined integration therefore remain pending.

These runs use the known archive snapshot, bypass only the public admission gate
inside a private diagnostic, and keep physical validation active. They do not
prove live-latest preparation, ordinary public admission or export. The latest
normal fifteen-sector preparation retries failed externally during the archive
transfer/read; the previously verified export remains the operational baseline.

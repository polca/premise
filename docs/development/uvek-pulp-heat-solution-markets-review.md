# Active-reference solution markets

Three exact ecoinvent 3.9 cut-off markets now use on-demand native-background reconstructions for hydrogen peroxide in 50% solution, sodium hypochlorite in 15% solution and industrial phosphoric acid in 85% solution. Each remains per signed kg of active chemical, with factor +1. All 27 original producer/freight rows, quantities and supported uncertainty remain separate and unrenormalized. Source exchange hashes were verified independently against private evidence.

Ecoinvent's [2013 methodology, section 9.3, printed pages 60–61](https://support.ecoinvent.org/hubfs/Knowledge%20Base/Database/Fundamentals/dataqualityguideline_ecoinvent_3_20130506.pdf#page=68) explains the active-substance reference convention and the older solution names. The percentages describe the supplied concentration; they do not justify inverse-concentration multipliers. Native archive reference identities retain that legacy naming and positive 1 kg references. Matching hydrogen demand (0.0656 kg) for peroxide and matching phosphoric feed/sulfuric-acid quantities (1.02/0.129 kg) corroborate the nominal basis. The earlier native active-chemical inspection independently supports phosphoric acid's active reference.

Hypochlorite is an explicit inventory-boundary/reagent screening proxy. Both descriptions refer to chlorine-emission capture, but the source inventories record 0.952447874 kg primary chlorine and 1.074419485 kg active caustic per kg. Native UVEK records no primary chlorine supply and 0.56 kg active caustic. Native heat/electricity, allocation and emissions also differ. The complete native stock is used at its active reference; no reagent-fitting scale, primary-chlorine compensation or avoided-emission credit is invented. The effect of missing primary-chlorine burdens and other differences on scores is unquantified. This proxy is not an exact reconstruction of the source producer inventories.

Source regional producer inventories use complete native RER supplies. Original final-product rail, road, barge and container-sea freight remains at its original tonne-km. Native plants already contain precursor transport. Country, generation/traction, fleet/load, grade, technology, allocation, vintage, internal uncertainty and biosphere-exclusion differences remain documented screening limitations. No extra water, transport concentration, purity, density, loss or carbon factor is added. Separate caller emissions and carbon-resource exchanges remain unchanged.

The existing 3.8 phosphoric-acid mapping retains its native direct target and factor +1. Its positive active basis is now explicit in the rule metadata; its old approval fingerprint was checked before refresh, and its original review evidence remains unchanged. Tests verify identical scientific rule coordinates and direct migrated quantities, uncertainty and formulas without helpers. No other source release, consequential or reverse route is newly approved.

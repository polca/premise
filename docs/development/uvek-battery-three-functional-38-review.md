# Three functional native battery proxies

The real battery inventory retry now stops at two unapproved source-3.8
correspondences: manganese sulfate and vinyl carbonate, down from five.
Ordinary CLIC approval and source/target validation remain enabled. This is
preparation evidence, not a completed battery update or export. Fifteen of
nineteen sectors remain qualified.

Three exact source-3.8 cut-off scopes use complete existing native inventories
with factor +1 per signed kg:

- **Copper anode supply:** use global primary copper at refinery as the already
  established material screening proxy. All five actual battery callers have no
  separate refining input; rolling and other processing stay separate. Native
  electrorefining, SX-EW and refined-metal quality differ from the anode boundary.
  Do not subtract guessed refining burdens or invent purity/yield adjustments.
  Other callers with downstream refining need a separate boundary assessment.
- **Synthetic battery-grade graphite:** use the complete Chinese battery-grade
  graphite inventory as a functional carbon proxy. The source graphitizes
  amorphous carbon. Native documentation instead describes mining/crushing/milling
  approximations, with additional coke and electricity for battery grade. These
  manufacturing routes are not equivalent. The warning explicitly retains that
  technology and energy difference, uncertain performance and unquantified score
  effects. No guessed graphitization operation or electricity factor is added.
- **RoW latex:** use the complete European styrene-butadiene dispersion aggregate.
  The inspected source producer and native process both inherit the APME survey
  of seventeen European plants and emulsion polymerization of the same monomers.
  Nominal kg is retained without guessing solids content or a wet/dry factor.
  Native production at plant replaces the complete source delivered market;
  final freight, geography, vintage and identical grade are not claimed preserved.
  The nonempty aggregate has no direct technosphere inputs; embedded utilities
  are included but cannot be individually relinked to IAM markets.

Caller signs, formulas, supported uncertainty and separately listed manufacturing
and emissions remain unchanged. All proxy, allocation, vintage, geography,
internal-uncertainty and documented BAFU biosphere-coverage differences remain
explicit, with unquantified impact effects. The earlier copper source-3.10 target,
factor and sign decision remain unchanged. There is no new helper, foreground
omission, upstream reconstruction or source-code edit.

Verification includes eight direct conversion cases with 24 signed normal/
lognormal exchange checks, fresh and warm public harmonization, input

backgrounds and stay limited to necessary immediate manufacturing operations.

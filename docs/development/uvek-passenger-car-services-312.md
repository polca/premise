# Car treatment, freight and positive-reference supplies

Eleven further exact source-3.12 approvals reduce the first car-import cohort
from eighteen refusals to seven. This is still a partial import, not a verified
car update or a count of every later source-version cohort.

Five existing treatment rules already have the correct factor minus one. The
review independently confirms negative source references and positive native
references for brake, road and tyre wear, electric-powertrain dismantling and
industrial-electronics-plastic incineration. Those five migration rules remain
unchanged. Negative waste demand remains positive demand for treatment; it does
not become an emissions credit. Native wear partitioning, dismantling boundaries
and incineration/recovery differences are warned. The plastic incinerator's title
and composition text disagree about moisture; no speculative correction is made.

Two freight candidates change. European rail freight now uses complete native
European rail freight, retaining rail operation, shunting and infrastructure,
instead of the incorrect road-freight candidate. The EURO6 16–32 t road market
uses the native European 26 t EURO-VI long-haul service. Different payloads,
lifetime and duty cycle remain explicit limitations, without multiplying the
caller tonne-kilometres again.

Four existing profile guards gain independently reviewed 3.12 entries: pure
sulphuric acid, cobalt metal, molybdenum metal and unsaturated polyester resin.
Their native references and product basis are checked. Weak native production
analogies, allocation, missing source detail and market delivery differences
remain warnings. All previously approved effective profile rules are compared
with their prior versions before their enclosing fingerprints are rebound.

No runtime Python, helper dataset or new source omission is introduced. The six
actual rule edits and their maintainer replay are mapping-resource maintenance.
Six focused Brightpath tests pass, covering signed demand, uncertainty, native
releases, old profile decisions and atomic/idempotent replay. Four Premise
approval-integrity checks pass. Clean generation reproduces all three packaged
forward resources byte for byte. No Brightway database is written.

The remaining cohort includes merchant hydrogen, quicklime and five existing
recipe correspondences. The merchant-hydrogen source is actually 200 bar, so
directly replacing it with 25-bar native hydrogen would lose compression.
That pressure difference has not been approved as equivalent.

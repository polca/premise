# Waste-service boundaries within the nitrate supply chain

The inspection covers the waste suppliers of the unchanged nine-root,
55-record electricity pilot. A fresh read-only inspection contains 319 complete
source/native inventories for the 49 waste identities outstanding at that
checkpoint, their linked treatment suppliers and existing helper primitives.
Finding a target identity in both native releases does not establish a valid
source treatment mix or scientific equivalence.

The exact ecoinvent 3.9 Swiss waste-concrete market now independently reuses
the existing reviewed inert-landfill reconstruction. Fresh market, treatment
and generic landfill-component hashes match the earlier complete-inventory
witnesses. The helper retains the as-received reference, landfill operation,
original infrastructure share and both separate collection services, including
their uncertainty. All three helper records are returned and included in LCI.
The negative source waste reference becomes positive treatment demand through
factor -1; no demolition, crushing, dry-mass scaling or recovery credit is added.

Native generic landfill components match the earlier UVEK 2025 witnesses and
retain the same technosphere vectors in 2026 within serialization precision.
Their internal heat, materials, transport, emissions and allocation remain
complete, with the already reviewed screening differences. Full 2026 facility
metadata additionally describes monitoring after closure. This supplies
information absent from the older abbreviated readback; it does not prove equal
source/native operating or aftercare lifetimes. The packaged pilot review pins
the raw rule, every recipe in its closure, the earlier review and fresh source
and native hashes. No migration rule or recipe changes.

Other findings remain work items, not admissions:

- Regional concrete and final-disposal mineral-wool services use generic
  inert-landfill operation and infrastructure. Their source references,
  uncertainty, delegated component markets and geographic boundaries must be
  preserved before admitting their helpers.
- Mineral-wool recycling contains explicit dismantling dust emissions. It
  cannot be treated as an empty cut-off handover. Sorting and collection branches
  also retain their own operations and final-disposal links.
- Regional glass, polyethylene and polystyrene markets combine open burning,
  open dumps, unsanitary landfills and, in some regions, controlled incineration.
  Their infiltration classes and fate weights cannot be replaced by a single
  Swiss incinerator. European market groups delegate to country-specific mixes;
  Swiss fly-ash extraction remains a distinct technology boundary.
- Hazardous-waste markets explicitly split energy-recovery and other
  incineration services. Waste-paint markets additionally contain landfill,
  clinker and, in Switzerland, fly-ash-extraction branches. Preserve these
  allocations rather than inferring one generic fate from the market name.
- Average-wastewater markets include sewer infrastructure, direct elementary
  releases and country-specific treated fractions. A complete native treatment
  process alone does not represent the whole source market.
- The inspected reinforcement-steel recycling service contains dismantling
  energy only. Its source cut-off boundary does not authorize adding remelting
  or an avoided-primary-steel credit. Collection and sorting are separate
  services with additional exchanges.

The solvent-based alkyd paint inspection also finds a substantive boundary
difference: the source reference excludes bulk solvent, while the native wet
mixture includes white-spirit supply. Applying the previously approved
water-based paint concentration factor alone would introduce extra solvent
burdens. Both solvent-based market correspondences remain unresolved; there is
no automatic extension of that earlier paint decision.

# Native wind and solar material mappings

The existing shared material update now applies 29 rules to 19 actual native
wind, solar and stack inventories in the combined physical graph, with no
missing-provider errors. All 38 signed inventory calculations for those targets
are finite and signed linearity passes. No upstream process, wrapper, helper or
inventory root was added. The installed complete workflow/export is still a
separate qualification step.

Wind aliases identify seven original moving-parts assemblies and their seven
existing direct-drive variants. The approved rule selector applies only the
existing neodymium rule, with each turbine's stated rated MW per unit. Other
whole-plant bulk-metal totals do not describe those components. Four assemblies
already obtain neodymium through complete magnets and are preserved. The other
ten receive the existing oxide/metal factor and complete native Chinese supply.
No compatible native dysprosium-oxide carrier is present, so no concentrate or
other element is substituted for it.

Native CIS, generic CdTe and micro-Si references are positive m2. Native source
documentation gives 78 Wp / 0.72 m2 for CIS, 84 Wp / 0.72 m2 for generic CdTe in
DE/MY/US, and 100 Wp / 1.4 m2 for micro-Si. These become MW per m2 directly.
The native CdTe basis differs from the older 65 Wp ecoinvent conversion; Series
4/6 inventories are outside this generic selector. Micro-Si documentation also
states 10% efficiency, which is inconsistent with its quoted power and area;
the mapping follows the quoted rating and area, as the original conversion did.

CIS keeps the existing CdS, gallium, indium and selenium formulas; CdTe keeps its
CdTe stoichiometry and projected indium input. Micro-Si remains the existing
representative for the a-Si category, with its original Ge/indium overlays. These
are material-demand forecasts, not complete future layer-stack reconstructions.
The inherited gallium factor of three and all other original rule factors stay
unchanged. Complete existing supplies retain their kg references and declared
US/RER/GLO geography; regional choices warn through the approved lookup. Source
technology, delivery, allocation, vintage, uncertainty and unquantified score
differences remain explicit limits. Existing native reference products allow
old direct inputs to be replaced without keeping a duplicate metal input.

Native crystalline-Si cells retain silver in metallization pastes under the same
preservation approach as the existing IEA PVPS cell policies. The complete tandem
panel also remains unchanged: its ITO arrives through a shared LCD sputtering
service in m3, not the direct nanoscale target powder expected by the perovskite
rule. A panel-level powder addition would overlap the existing coating; changing
the shared LCD service would affect other products.

No native SOFC manufacturing stack or compatible pure yttrium-oxide carrier was
found. Cerium supply is a 60% oxide concentrate, so the matched SOEC ceramic
design remains unchanged as a group. ICEV, nuclear and CSP intensity data end
at 2020; they are not extrapolated to 2050. The existing motor-component scope
and missing-intensity guards remain. These are explicit source/method limits,
not successful forecasts of those absent designs.

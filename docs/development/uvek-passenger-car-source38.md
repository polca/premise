# Six required source-3.8 car inputs

The actual car import reached six unapproved source-3.8 correspondences after
clearing the reviewed source-3.10 cohort. All six have native candidates with
matching positive one-kg references, independently checked in the installed
source database and both native releases. No migration rule, helper recipe or
runtime Python change is needed. Exact source-3.8 approvals retain the following
limitations in the conversion warnings and audit.

Chromium-steel metalworking excludes the principal steel input and degreasing.
Both inventories include 0.227 kg of replacement steel for processing losses,
plus machine, factory and operation. Principal stock and any caller degreasing
remain separate. Liquid epoxy is an uncured bisphenol-A/epichlorohydrin resin
in both models, but the aggregated native eco-profile replaces the newer source
unit-process model. Different CAS labels do not establish identical formulation
or polymer grade; no hardener or curing service is introduced.

Native European glass-fibre manufacture uses the documented recuperative or
oxy-fuel furnace technology. It replaces the global producer and delivery mix.
Native HDPE granulate uses its complete European eco-profile, including upstream
feedstock, waste treatment and the documented 2023 methane update.

Flexible polyurethane uses the complete native TDI/polyol composition rather
than the source market's 20% MDI-based and 80% TDI-based mix. Wrought aluminium
uses the native European production mix: 0.53644 kg primary, 0.38099 kg new-scrap
secondary and 0.082568 kg old-scrap secondary per kg. Source global recycling
shares, specific alloy grade and delivery are not reproduced. These two cases
are screening replacements for complete markets, not preservation of their
internal producer shares or freight.

All caller quantities, signs, formulas and uncertainty remain unchanged. No
density, volume, lifetime, occupancy, recovery-credit or energy multiplier is
introduced. Model allocation, geography, vintage, internal uncertainty and
environmental score differences remain explicit and unquantified. Existing
native biosphere exclusions are not repaired by these correspondences.

with uncertainty and source-ownership checks. The complete car workflow still
requires its normal shared update, physical validation, combined scenario and
finite LCIs before sector qualification. No Brightway database is written by

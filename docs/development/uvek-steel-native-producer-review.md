# Native steel producer aliases

The standard Steel class now receives the selected background family and biosphere version. Previously it always initialized ecoinvent mappings, making UVEK YAML aliases ineffective. This small generic propagation changes two Python modules; the transformation order, IAM data, technology shares, market construction and efficiency formulas remain shared. Ecoinvent remains the default. Public steel support remains gated.

The steel YAML selects two native converter processes (unalloyed and low-alloyed, RER) and three electric processes (unalloyed and low-alloyed, CH; combined un- and low-alloyed, RER). All five installed native records have a positive one-kilogram reference. Converter inputs include pig iron; electric inputs include iron scrap and electricity. Exact name, product and kilogram filters exclude chromium/alloy-specialty grades, reinforcing-steel imports, hot rolling, disposal and best/worst-plant sensitivity variants. These historical CH/RER inventories remain regional technology proxies; their operating conditions, grade mix and geographic extrapolation have unquantified score effects.

The native “Steel, low-alloyed, at plant” record mixes converter/electric supply and includes hot rolling. It is deliberately excluded from the producer aliases. It must not be treated as a bare supply market in a way that removes rolling. Native market/consumer integration remains unfinished.

Validation evidence: 64 focused tests pass, including existing ecoinvent grade and market behavior and new UVEK inclusion/exclusion and family-routing tests. The real encrypted REMIND SSP2-NPi 2050 diagnostic again prepares 138 imports, then passes the former missing-secondary-supplier failure and reaches strict steel validation. It stops with 103 findings: 65 pig-iron energy, 12 secondary-steel share, and 26 pig-iron input findings. The legacy checks contain ecoinvent-specific names; actual exchanges must still be audited before identifying findings as naming mismatches or physical defects. No checks were disabled or thresholds relaxed. No steel export is claimed.

The next work is the shared validator/filter and native consumer audit, including pig-iron regionalization, fuel-name recognition, grade-consistent market inputs, retention of rolling, and finite inventories through export. This commit is a reviewed prerequisite, not completed sector support.

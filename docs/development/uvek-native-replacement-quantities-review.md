# Fill native workbook quantities before importing them

The UVEK retained-workbook path previously skipped Premise's ordinary replacement-quantity step. Licensed workbook entries tagged with a replacement source are lookup requests, often stored as zero. Importing those zeros discarded real material and fuel inputs. Steel validation exposed this problem; accepting different supplier names alone would not repair it.

The new mapping file, `premise/data/utils/import/native_replacement_quantities.yaml`, explicitly identifies the workbook activity, source profile, replacement source, native donor and each permitted lookup. The first qualified recipe covers the ecoinvent 3.9 cut-off blast-furnace-with-CCS template using native RER pig iron. Its 27 tagged source slots resolve to 12 native technosphere exchanges and seven direct native elementary exchanges. Repeated source gas, sludge and wastewater geographies are grouped by the existing Premise filler and do not duplicate the donor quantities. Gas uses its actual native MJ basis, not an invented amount in the unknown source m³ slot. Explicit non-placeholder exchanges, including slag and capture, remain unchanged.

Six source slots are absent from the selected donor: blast-furnace dust, packed fireclay refractory, dioxins in air, hydrogen sulfide in air, water in air, and unspecified natural-origin water. They are specifically recorded omissions under the standing autonomous modelling approval. Their source quantities are unknown, not established zeros, and their score effects remain unquantified. Native lead, manganese and nickel ionic labels are retained with their native signed quantities; no molecular-weight correction or equality with elemental source definitions is asserted. Historical technology, ore grade, geography, allocation and emissions differences remain screening limitations. The recipe samples the corresponding native input groups; it does not claim to copy the complete donor or reproduce the ecoinvent inventory.

A small generic bridge in `replacement_quantities.py` translates the reviewed lookups, then calls the unchanged `BaseInventoryImport.fill_dataset_data_gaps` method. This Python change is necessary because YAML alone cannot invoke the operation skipped by the UVEK branch. It adds no sector transformation engine or new numerical conversion formula. Native technosphere purchases use the existing boundary-anchor mechanism so they are not migrated again as ecoinvent inputs. Already linked native elementary flows are restored after source-profile migration and retain the selected installed biosphere links. Records and native databases remain read-only until all lookups in the owned batch qualify; unsupported donors or changed placeholders fail clearly.

Each applied recipe emits a warning and records the actual filled exchanges, omissions, native donor, review fingerprint and limitations in provenance and diagnostics. Missing quantities cannot silently pass as physical zeros. The cache fingerprint includes the filled native exchanges and elementary flows, and cache reuse cannot bypass a withdrawn recipe. Source placeholders are deterministic; the ordinary filler transfers the selected native quantities, not donor exchange uncertainty distributions. Upstream provider inventories remain unchanged. Source chemical-formula metadata remains unchanged; meaningful uncertainty or altered formulas require a separate review rather than invented scaling from zero.

Validation passed: 38 focused and workbook/user/hybrid regression tests; two signed finite LCI calculations for the newly filled native components, including direct elementary flows; and real public preparation of biomass, electricity, heat and cement, importing 632 records without changing the native database. The component LCI excludes the explicit workbook exchanges and therefore does not establish a complete CCS inventory, steel transformation, certification or export. No full four-sector transformation/export rerun is claimed for this preparation check.

Steel now stops earlier and more accurately at the next unqualified record, ordinary low-alloyed BF/BOF steel. A workbook-wide metadata audit finds 28 activities with 641 replacement slots referring to 11 distinct donor queries. These are repeated lookup fields, not 641 independent datasets or new missing migration correspondences. Only the first activity is qualified in this commit; the remaining donor/lookup mappings, regional pig-iron links, gas classification, strict steel validation and export remain unfinished. Earlier reports of successful steel inventory preparation did not establish that these quantities had been filled. See `uvek-steel-replacement-metadata-audit.json` for the bounded metadata scan.

The existing four supported sectors still prepare successfully. The steel public support gate remains closed. Other sectors, known full-suite failures and source limitations remain part of the active integration goal.

How the metals corrections changed RELICS scores

1 kg of each market product · IMAGE SSP2-M, 2050 · ecoinvent 3.12 · metals-only update

Historical comparison before the cobalt/zinc repair. The follow-up results show the correction.

Before and after refer to the code corrections, with the same scenario and source inventory. Values are kg of the named RELICS resource extracted per kg of market product, including its supply chain. Ores and compounds are not normalized to a kilogram of contained metal. Mixed products have separate resource rows; the indicators are not summed.

A decrease is a change in resource accounting and supply, not evidence of a real-world improvement. The table selects the product-associated indicators. The complete matrices also include other resources used upstream.

Attribution gaps found: cobalt falls to effectively zero and zinc falls by about 95%, although their markets remain almost entirely primary supply. Those decreases were accounting gaps, not resource savings. Consequential sulfur also needs separate attribution review.
